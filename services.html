Grading

Pattern Grading is often associated with complicated calculations and laborious work in order to scale a pattern into different sizes. But, we use fully automated Pattern Grading Systems in order to construct garment patterns according to the size indicated by the client. Patterns are graded as per size table sent by client or proposed by us.

The grading process uses multi-dimension size tables like waists, waists lengths and type of figure it is important in case when ease to operate size in the model to keep correct shape and matches the customer’s requirement which is done is different CAD Systems.

Marker Planning

marker is a thin piece of paper that contains all the required pattern pieces of all sizes for a specific style of garments. A marker is a diagram of a precise arrangement of pattern pieces for sizes of a specific style that are to be cut from in one spread.

So, a maker is a schematic of the graded pattern pieces that is used as a cutting guide. When produced by experienced marker makers, a marker will optimize the usage of your fabric and improve overall cutting quality.

Cut Planning & Lay Planning

Cut Planning & Lay Planning are the most essential managing process of cutting room, table space and labor. To do accurate fabric spreading and cutting, reduce manufacturing cost with schedule time, minimize the fabric wastage manufacturer make a lay plan for garment manufacturing.

The cutting templates representing all of the individual components of a garment have to be laid out together in such a way that they fit within the confines of the fabric width as closely and efficiently as possible, in order to minimize waste.

Computerized Pattern cutting & printing

This is a service that digitizes paper templates into digital form. Customer delivers paper patterns and is given them back as a computer files. If there is such need and customer orders such service. We are making any necessary corrections and amendments.

All digitized patterns contain necessary information’s as name, size, additional points, lines, notches, markers. Such prepared patterns are ready to be used for production.
